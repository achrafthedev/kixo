Features

Everything Kick chat should have been.

Built for chatters who follow ten streams, mods who need to act fast, and streamers who want chat on top of their game.

All your chats, one window

Tabs for every channel with live dots and unread counters. Drag to reorder, Ctrl+1-9 to jump.

Kick + 7TV emotes

Channel and global emotes from Kick and 7TV, a searchable picker, :emote autocomplete and Tab-completion.

Mod tools that keep up

Hover a message to delete or time out. User cards show account age, follow date, sub months and recent messages.

Never miss a mention

A Mentions tab across every channel, custom highlight words, sound + desktop notifications.

Clean chat, your rules

Hide bots, ignore users, search chat with Ctrl+F. Chat pauses when you scroll up — no more losing your place.

Fast & light

One connection for all channels, batched rendering, ~300 KB of UI. Smooth even in the busiest chats.

Make it yours

Dark, Midnight (OLED), Slate and Light themes, any accent colour, font & emote size, compact mode.

Official Kick API

Login, sending and moderation use Kick's official OAuth API. Tokens are encrypted by your OS keychain.

Always up to date

Automatic updates in the background. Free forever, open source under MIT.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Kixo free?

Yes — free forever and open source under the MIT license. No ads, no tracking.

Do I need a Kick account?

Not to read chat. Log in with Kick when you want to send messages, moderate, or edit your stream info.

Is it safe? Does it use the official API?

Sending messages, moderation and stream edits go through Kick's official Public API with OAuth 2.1. Kixo never sees your password, and tokens are encrypted with your operating system's keychain. The live chat feed uses the same public stream kick.com shows logged-out viewers.

Does it support 7TV emotes?

Yes — channel and global 7TV emotes render automatically next to Kick emotes. You can turn them off in Settings.

How do I add chat to OBS?

Use the overlay builder above, or add a Browser Source in OBS with https://kixo.app/overlay?channel=yourname. No install or account needed. Options like &fade=30, &size=18, &bg=1 and &bots=0 let you tune it.

Is Kixo made by Kick?

No. Kixo is an independent community project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Kick.
